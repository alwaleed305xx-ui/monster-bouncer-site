Privacy Policy

Monster Bouncer · Last updated: 2026-10-25 · Applies to the Monster Bouncer app for iPhone and to the Monster Bouncer web version on this website.

In short
  • The app collects no data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • Nothing is shared unless you tap Share, and then only through your device's own share sheet, to wherever you choose.

1. Who we are

Monster Bouncer ("we") is a single-player game made by an independent developer, shown as the seller on the game's App Store page. For questions, see Contact (section 12).

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software. We do not run a server that receives data from the game, and we do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

3. Data stored on your device

To keep your game, the app saves it on your device only: in its local storage under keys that start with mbouncer. and, in the iPhone app, also in the app's own storage on the device, so that a cleared web cache does not lose what you bought. The save holds:

  • your progress (nights played and their best results, stars, the House Band, medals, the Daily Night streak and the All-Nighter best);
  • your gems, boosters, outfits, party themes and skins, and the state of the piggy bank and the Club Pass;
  • a record of your purchases (product, transaction ID and date), so that each one is delivered exactly once and can be restored;
  • your settings, such as sound, music, vibration, left-hand mode and language.

This data stays on your device and is never sent to us, so we cannot see, recover or move it. Deleting the app deletes it from your device. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup, which Apple runs for you, may include it, depending on your settings; if it does, your progress can come back when you restore that backup. We never receive these backups. Settings → Reset progress erases your nights, band and streak; your gems and purchases stay (see the Terms of Use).

4. In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases in the iPhone app use Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes the payment and handles your Apple Account and payment details; we never receive your name, email address, card or bank details. To deliver what you bought, the app receives from Apple only the product, a transaction ID and the purchase date, and keeps them on your device so that nothing is delivered twice. They are not sent to us. We do not use any third-party purchase, receipt or subscription service. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Sharing and challenge links

The game shares something only when you tap Share or Challenge a friend. It then opens your device's system share sheet, and you choose the app and the person, or cancel. What is shared is a picture of your result card and a link to the same night: the night number or date, a number that picks the same guests (the "seed") and your score. It contains no name, account or device information. We do not receive anything you share. In a browser that has no share sheet, the web version saves the picture as a download and copies the text and link to your clipboard instead.

6. Advertising

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising software. It does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for permission to track you. If we ever add ads to a future version, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

7. Network use

There are no analytics. The app makes no network requests of its own: its only connection is to Apple when you view prices, buy or restore purchases. The Terms and Privacy links in the game open this website in your browser, outside the game.

8. Fonts

The fonts (Baloo 2, Poppins and Cairo, under the SIL Open Font License) are bundled inside the app, the web version and this website. Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts or any other font service.

9. Web version and this website

If you play the web version in a browser, it saves your progress in that browser's local storage and keeps an offline copy of the game's own files (a service worker). It takes no payments: items bought with gems work as in the app, and paid items show "Purchases are in the app", because they can only be bought in the iPhone app, through Apple. Nothing is sent anywhere.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). If you contact us through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username and what you write are public and handled under GitHub's terms, so please do not post personal information there.

10. Children

Monster Bouncer is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect none from children either. Purchases always need the approval of the Apple Account holder; parents can control them with Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

11. Your rights (PDPL and GDPR)

If you live in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) gives you rights over your personal data, including the rights to be informed, to access, to correct and to request deletion. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the GDPR / UK GDPR gives you similar rights, including the right to object.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete: your game data is only on your device, and you can delete it at any time by deleting the app (and its copy in your iCloud Backup, if you use one). Purchase records are held by Apple (privacy.apple.com). You can still send us any privacy question or request (section 12), and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Saudi Arabia, the Saudi Data and AI Authority, SDAIA).

12. Contact

Support page: alwaleed305xx-ui.github.io/monster-bouncer-site/support.html
Privacy questions and requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app would collect any data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
