A night at the door

Real screens from the game. Everything you see is drawn in code, in one sticker style.

How the door works

One poster, one rope, one swipe per guest. Easy to learn, and the monsters get sneakier every night.

1

Read the poster

A guest gets in only if it matches every picture on the poster. A green ring means it must have that; a red slash means it must not.

2

Swipe IN or OUT

OUT←→IN

Swipe right or tap IN to let a guest in, swipe left or tap OUT to bounce it. No clock, but guests do run out of patience, and three wrong admits shut the party down.

3

Bust the fakes

tape

Liars tape on fake eyes, paper hats and tied-on tails that wobble too much, and shapeshifters change one thing right before the rope. Only what is real counts.

More than one night

Story nights that keep getting trickier, plus a few ways to come back every day.

Daily Night

The same guests for everyone, once a day. Keep your streak going.

All-Nighter

The endless shift. The rules keep changing; how long can you hold the door?

Challenge a friend

Share a link and your friend gets the exact same guests. Beat their score.

House Band

Five monsters, five instruments. Dress them in outfits and pick a party theme.

Boosters

X-Ray Glasses, Slow-Mo and Second Chance help on story nights. Never in the Daily.

Fair by design

No ads, no energy, no timers on offers and nothing random for sale. Progress stays on your phone.

الليلة الباب بإيدك

انت البودي قارد في سهرة الوحوش. شوف البوستر، اكشف المزيّفين، واسحب كل ضيف: يمين يدخل، يسار برّا. وكل قرار صح يضيف آلة موسيقية لفرقة البيت فوق، فتسمع بإذنك كيف ماشي أداؤك.

  • البوستر: الدائرة الخضراء يعني لازم يكون عنده الشي، والشطبة الحمراء يعني ممنوع.
  • المزيّفين: عيون ملصوقة وقبعات ورق وذيول مربوطة تهتز زيادة عن اللزوم، وما تنحسب.
  • المتحوّلين: يغيّرون شي واحد قبل الحبل بلحظة. احكم على شكله الجديد.
  • ليلة اليوم والسهرة المفتوحة وتحدّي صاحبك بنفس الضيوف بالضبط.
  • بدون إعلانات، بدون طاقة، وتقدّمك محفوظ على جوالك.
