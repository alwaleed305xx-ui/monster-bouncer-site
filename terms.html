2. The game

Monster Bouncer is a single-player game: you are the doorman of a monster party and decide which guests get in. It can be downloaded and played without paying; optional in-app purchases are available. There is no multiplayer, no chat and no user-generated content. A challenge link only gives a friend the same guests to play on their own device. An internet connection is needed only for purchases.

3. Virtual items

Gems, boosters, outfits, party themes, rope, door and button skins, the Club Pass, the VIP Rope, the Gold Star Road, the piggy bank and every other item in the game are virtual items. They are licensed to you for use inside the game only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, sold, transferred or moved to another game. Gems and boosters do not expire; each booster can be held up to 99, and boosters that would go past that cap are added as gems instead.

They can never be used in the Daily Night, the All-Nighter or a friend's challenge. "Boss, Reopen!" lets you continue a story night that was shut down, for gems (the first one is free); a reopened night can score at most Bronze, and playing the night again is always free.
